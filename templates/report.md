# DD/MM/YYYY - W## · <stage>

**Period:** <Monday> to <Sunday> · **Source:** <CRM object> · **Owner:** <name>

> One or two lines: how big the week was, and the caveat that people from this week are still inside their decision window, so their conversion is not scored yet.

## 1. In one line
The actionable headline, before any number. Example: "The money is not where the noise is: price is the loudest and converts 0%; the fast money is in the broken checkout and in people who did not know it was paid."

## 2. The week in numbers
| Week | Replies in this stage | Total replies | People entering the stage |
|---|---|---|---|

Two or three bullets on how to read it (batches, holidays, a new email that changed volume).

## 3. The main concerns
One block per concern, most common first. Each block:
> "Literal quote." (email or id)

- **What it is:** plain language.
- **How many people:** unique people this week.
- **Do they end up converting / completing?** From the baseline or the mature cohort.
- **What it means:** one or two lines.

If a concern has several distinct technical signatures, add a small table: what breaks, how many, who.

## 4. Where the money actually is
The same concerns, reordered by how many convert (or complete), not by volume. Say plainly where they differ.

## 5. What we reply, and whether it works
| Concern | Our standard reply | % that converts | Does the reply work? |
|---|---|---|---|

Plus one real worked example (problem, our reply, outcome).

## 6. Product bug radar (process stages only)
The two weights, the lethality, the clusters table (name, people, funnel position, tier, repro line) and which one to escalate today.

## 7. Workflow health (process stages only)
Entered the stage this week, still there, moved on, dropped. State that it is reconstructed from cohorts if the CRM does not expose workflows.

## 8. Are we aiming at the right thing?
Each concern, the initiative that addresses it, and a verdict: aligned, blind spot (volume, recoverable, no owner) or cannot be validated with this data.

## 9. What to watch every week
Four or five numbers to iterate on.

## 10. Decisions
Recommendations written as decisions, each with an owner.

## 11. How we measure this
Plain version: who we count and how, what was dropped as noise and by which rule, the rigor checks and their numbers (stage leakage, survivorship, unidentified contacts), and what we cannot see.
